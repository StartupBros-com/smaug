The Mochary Method Curriculum is a publicly shared Google Docs program for founders and operators. It directs readers to make a personal copy of the curriculum, work through its sub-documents, and record their answers to prompts as they go.

The bookmarked recommendation presents the materials as executive coach Matt Mochary’s open-sourced curriculum for entrepreneurial leadership. It offers a self-directed starting point for applying coaching concepts that are often delivered through private advisory relationships.
