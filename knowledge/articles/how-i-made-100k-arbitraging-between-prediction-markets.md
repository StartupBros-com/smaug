This practitioner-authored guide presents a self-reported $100,000 approach to cross-venue prediction-market arbitrage. It explains how to buy every outcome at the lowest available price across platforms when their combined cost is below $1, then judge whether the spread compensates for capital being tied up until resolution. The author emphasizes that execution speed, liquidity preparation, automated monitoring, exit opportunities, fees, and resolution criteria determine whether an apparent spread can actually be captured.

## Key Takeaways

- Compare equivalent markets across venues and buy the cheapest contract for each mutually exclusive outcome only when the basket costs less than its $1 payout.
- Multi-outcome and smaller markets can create more pricing discrepancies because their complexity, thin liquidity, and platform-specific fees make prices harder to reconcile.
- Rank opportunities by annualized return: a modest spread may not be attractive if settlement is weeks away, while the same spread resolving quickly can justify deploying capital.
- Price divergences are short-lived, often following rumors or uneven market updates, so monitoring several platforms and keeping funds available is central to the proposed strategy.
- The guide advocates exiting a basket before resolution when its combined resale value rises, while warning readers to account for order-book depth, fees, and exact resolution rules.
