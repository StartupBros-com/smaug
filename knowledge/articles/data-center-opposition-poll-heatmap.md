A Heatmap Pro poll conducted by Embold Research finds that three-quarters of Americans would oppose a data center being built near where they live, with 61% saying they would strongly oppose it. Using unchanged question wording, Heatmap tracked a sharp swing in opinion over a year: support and opposition were split 43-42 a year ago, a bare majority opposed in February, and opposition has now reached 75%. The story also covers party ID and rural/urban breakdowns (only the headline and lede were captured in the extracted content).

## Key Takeaways

- 75% of Americans would oppose a nearby data center; 61% would strongly oppose.
- A year earlier the same question split 43-42 in favor of support, so the shift is rapid and large.
- Opposition crossed into a majority in February and kept climbing through the summer.
- Local siting politics are becoming a real constraint on AI infrastructure buildout.
