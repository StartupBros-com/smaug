A promotional playbook for building a faceless GTA 6 YouTube channel before the game's launch. It proposes using Claude with Higgsfield to turn trailer or gameplay footage into vertical Shorts, then using additional prompts to create high-energy narration, thumbnails, and a 30-day publishing plan. The article positions rapid AI-assisted production as a way to build an audience before the launch-time attention spike.

Its suggested monetization sequence is audience-building and digital products before launch, affiliate and sponsor revenue in launch week, then custom FiveM scripts and premium Discord communities afterward. The article is aspirational rather than evidence-led; the connector setup instruction supplies only the incomplete value `higgsfieldai`, so it should be independently verified before use.

## Key Takeaways

- Start publishing GTA 6-related Shorts before launch to establish an audience while competition is lower.
- Use one source video to generate multiple 9:16 clips, hook-focused voiceovers, subtitle direction, and thumbnail concepts.
- Refine generated scripts with retention prompts that add a strong opening, recurring stakes, and calls to action.
- Expand early experiments into a calendar covering Shorts, long-form videos, and community posts.
- The proposed revenue paths progress from digital products to affiliates, sponsorships, FiveM scripts, and paid communities.
