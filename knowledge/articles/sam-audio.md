SAM Audio is Meta AI’s unified sound-separation model for extracting a specified sound from complex audio or audio-visual mixtures. It accepts text, visual, or span prompts and is released alongside a perception encoder, benchmarks, and research papers to support experimentation and application development.

## Key Takeaways

- Text prompts can specify the sound to isolate from an audio or audio-visual source.
- The model also supports visual and span prompts for directing separation.
- Meta shared supporting benchmarks, research papers, and a perception encoder with the release.
