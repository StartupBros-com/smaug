This X Article is a playbook for solo creators selling AI-assisted video production to brands. The bookmark export provides the article title and preview rather than its full body; its accompanying post argues that brands accustomed to $50K-plus traditional shoots create room for creators who can deliver comparable work in roughly two days and charge $2K–$10K per engagement.

The advertised playbook covers which video models to learn and when to use them, a workflow for using multiple models from one workspace, and a fast route to building a portfolio and finding clients. Its core thesis is that client demand for AI-video execution currently exceeds the supply of practitioners who can operate the tools well.

## Key Takeaways

- Traditional production budgets can create a pricing umbrella for faster AI-assisted video services.
- The proposed service model centers on model selection, a unified production workflow, portfolio development, and client acquisition.
- The article frames the opportunity as time-sensitive because practical AI-video expertise remains scarce relative to interest from brands.
