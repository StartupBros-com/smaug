This X Article presents a retail-oriented set of prediction-market trading tactics based on market volatility rather than waiting for contracts to resolve. It argues that emotional news reactions can create short-lived mispricings, while trader analytics and outcome-range positions can help identify or construct trades with favorable payoff profiles. The author also cautions that superficially successful wallets may be hedged or delta-neutral, making indiscriminate copy trading unreliable.

## Key Takeaways

- The "sell the news" approach fades sharp, news-driven price moves after a market has time to cool, rather than chasing the initial reaction.
- Copy trading requires investigating a wallet's strategy and activity because apparent performance can conceal offsetting positions or hedges.
- In short, low-volatility crypto markets, spreading stakes across several likely price ranges is presented as a way to manage payoff probabilities.
- The article proposes that retail enthusiasm can distort long-shot contracts, creating opportunities for contrarian positions, but treats these as speculative strategies rather than guaranteed returns.
