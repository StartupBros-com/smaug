Rallies Arena describes an experiment called Rallies Arena that gave frontier AI models $100,000 portfolios and access to financial data. The article preview says the experiment was designed to test model investing; the accompanying bookmark claims that most models exceeded the S&P 500 while showing distinct portfolio-management styles.

The proposed product is an “AI Hedge Fund” agent that reviews the individual models' research, stress-tests their reasoning with additional data, and constructs a portfolio from their combined recommendations. This is a portfolio-level aggregation and verification approach, not simply a leaderboard for individual model returns; its performance claims require independent evaluation against benchmark methodology, costs, and live-market results.

## Key Takeaways

- The project uses model-managed portfolios to observe differences in investment behavior and research outputs.
- A supervising agent is intended to critique model reasoning and combine inputs into a single portfolio.
- The thesis anticipates fully automated hedge funds becoming competitive with conventional asset management, subject to live validation.
