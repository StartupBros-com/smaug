BBC News reports that Ghanaian influencer Abu Trica was extradited to the United States to face charges over an alleged scam targeting elderly Americans. The report says prosecutors accuse him of using romance scams to defraud victims of approximately $8 million, with alleged proceeds publicly displayed on Instagram.

The linked report is a useful counterpoint to the bookmark's claim that AI-mediated trust building becomes legitimate whenever it sells a real product. ## Key Takeaways

- The BBC reports an extradition connected to allegations of an $8 million romance-scam operation targeting older Americans.
- Romance scams exploit interpersonal trust and can impose both financial and emotional harm on victims.
