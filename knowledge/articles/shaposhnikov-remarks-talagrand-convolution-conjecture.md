arXiv paper (2609.11290) giving self-contained martingale proofs of Talagrand's convolution conjecture in the Gaussian and Boolean settings with explicit dimension-free constants. Bookmarked because Paata Ivanisvili cites it as the AI-assisted 7-page simplification of the original 40+ page proof, which he then got an AI to cut to 1.5 pages.

## Key Takeaways

- The argument analyzes the stopped density martingale and uses a weighted Ito isometry estimate.
- The Gaussian case was settled earlier by Eldan and Lee, and Lehec; the Boolean case was proved up to a log log factor by Chen and then settled by Lu, Guo and Fang.
- Context from the tweet: AI can increasingly shorten and restyle valid proofs on request.
