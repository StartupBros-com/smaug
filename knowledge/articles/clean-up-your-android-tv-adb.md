A short guide from Mert Cobanov for decluttering a sluggish Android TV over ADB without root. It offers a four-step setup plus a copy-paste prompt you hand to an AI agent, which disables the factory apps and ad rows you never use and swaps in a cleaner home screen launcher. Nothing is uninstalled and every step is reversible, and it works from Windows, Mac or Linux. Bookmarked via a French user who cleaned a TCL TV in about 25 minutes.

## Key Takeaways

- Uses ADB to disable (not uninstall) preinstalled apps and Google services that slow the TV down
- No root needed; all changes are reversible
- An AI agent is pointed at the TV with a ready-made prompt to do the cleanup
- Replace the stock home screen with a lighter launcher, removing ad rows
