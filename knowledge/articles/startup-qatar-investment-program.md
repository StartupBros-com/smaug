A QDB-backed program that funds tech startups to launch or expand in Qatar. The fetched page text was mostly empty, so details come from the bookmarking tweet.

## Key Takeaways

- START track: up to $1.1M for startups with a proof of concept or MVP.
- GROW track: up to $5.5M for established companies expanding.
- Perks cited: entrepreneur and flexible work visas, waived registration and license fees, subsidized housing and co-working, R&D grants, mentoring and hiring help.
- Open to any innovative startup, with priority sectors such as AI/ML, B2B SaaS, FinTech, HealthTech, cybersecurity, climate tech and robotics.
