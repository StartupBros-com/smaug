X announced an exclusive partnership with entrepreneur and media personality Khloé Kardashian for a 26-episode video podcast series scheduled to launch on the platform in fall 2024. The announcement positions the deal as part of X’s wider effort to make long-form, celebrity-led video a destination format rather than only a place for short social posts.

The supplied article text consists of its headline and preview, while the accompanying X Business post adds that the series will be exclusive to X. That exclusivity makes the partnership notable as a distribution experiment: X is using a recognizable creator and a recurring episodic format to test whether premium programming can attract and retain video audiences.

## Key Takeaways

- Khloé Kardashian’s planned series was announced as a 26-episode video podcast launching on X in fall 2024.
- The partnership is exclusive to X, making it a platform-distribution rather than merely promotional arrangement.
- X Business framed the project as evidence of the platform’s increased investment in video content and celebrity-led programming.
