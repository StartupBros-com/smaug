NEvo is a neural-guided evolutionary framework for synthesizing videos that maximally activate a selected visual brain region. Rather than selecting from existing stimuli, it computationally searches for generated visual sequences that drive the target region, offering a way to form testable hypotheses about what that region represents.

The project is described as an in-silico method for now. It complements other neuroscience approaches by turning a measured neural objective into visual stimuli that can be inspected, compared, and potentially used to guide later experiments.

## Key Takeaways

- NEvo targets arbitrary visual regions of interest with generated videos optimized for neural activation.
- Its search-based, fully computational approach can expose candidate visual features represented by a brain region.
- The method produces hypotheses from synthetic stimuli and does not itself establish what a region represents in biological experiments.
