Feed of numbered experiments from Every's frontier team, each with raw code and a short writeup. Dan Shipper suggests pointing your own agent at it to pick up ideas. Many entries are member-locked, so the index page only exposes titles and one-line summaries.

## Key Takeaways

- Entries are numbered (44 at the time of capture) with an owner, a stage such as "exploring", and a week
- Recent examples: Every Reader (your own Claude or Codex beside an article with comments in the margins), handing work to an agent via iPhone screen recordings picked up by a Mac mini, and map-driven autoresearch (a parent agent picks five of 104 research paths)
- Other titles include Heisentool (an agent-written fake Slack for testing agents), a storyboard-first video editor, and hill-climbing a writing skill on your own corrections
- Useful as an idea feed for agent workflows rather than a tutorial
