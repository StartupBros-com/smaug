Livsun's X article collapses every "seven streams of income" story into four multipliers: other people's time, money that earns, something that copies itself, and attention that keeps arriving. The frame is add vs multiply. Wages are addition — one body, one hour, a hard ceiling. Fortunes are multiplication — one unit of work or one dollar getting used more than once. Every fortune from a three-van operator to old money is some combination of these four and nothing else.

**Other people's time** is the oldest: buy an hour for less than it produces and keep the spread. A cleaner who hires two people at $120 and bills $200 is no longer the only pair of hands — but wages are owed whether the client pays, damage is yours, and you have swapped skilled work for sales and management. The tell: if you vanished for a month, does any work still get billed? Write the process down before you hire, or you now do the job and manage someone doing it badly.

**Money that earns** is the one everyone has heard of and almost nobody runs, because it has an entry fee. $300/month at a long-run 8% index left alone is ~$55k at 10 years ($36k contributed), ~$177k at 20 ($72k in), ~$447k at 30 ($108k in) — roughly $339k made by the money itself, almost all of it in the last decade. It cannot solve a money problem this year, the smooth chart lies about the experience (drawdowns of a third), and the real cost is stomach. The move that survives contact with life is an automatic payday transfer, not "I will invest properly once I earn more."

**Something that copies** is the rule-breaker: software, a $30 template sold a hundred times, a checklist, a contract pack. The ten-thousandth copy costs the same as the first. Building was never the hard part; being found was — which is why this multiplier is lethal paired with the fourth. Start from the thing you have already rebuilt or explained fifteen times.

**Attention that keeps arriving** is the only one that costs nothing to begin. A plumber posting one real-job video a week stops renting leads from a platform. Most of what you make reaches nobody; it only works with volume and time; attention with nothing to sell converts to zero; and it is rented land, which is why serious people move the audience to an email list they own.

The split that matters more than the four: the first two need permission and a pile (cash flow to pay people, surplus to invest). The last two need neither. From a standing start the ladder is hands → attention → something that copies → other people's time → money that earns. Most people try to jump to the last rung and conclude the whole thing is a lie. Stacked, the four stop being mysterious: the course-at-3am is attention plus copies; old money is money plus other people's time, quietly, for generations; the ones who look like they broke the game are running three or four wired together. There is no fifth thing.

## Key Takeaways

- Only four multipliers exist: other people's time, money that earns, something that copies, attention that keeps arriving
- Adding (wages) has a 24-hour ceiling; multiplying is one unit of work or money used more than once
- The first two require capital or cash flow; the last two are free and are the only honest start from zero
- Copy-once products fail because nobody finds them; attention fails because there is nothing to sell — they only work as a pair
- Automatic investing beats willpower; hiring before the job is written down turns a second pair of hands into a second problem
- If you vanished for a month and nothing still bills, you are not running other people's time, no matter how many people you know
