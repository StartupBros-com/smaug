Addy Osmani's Elevate essay arguing that once agents write the code, quality is no longer a post-hoc human review habit — it is the constraints you put around those agents. The captured page was HTML chrome only (title, author, og description); the body was not in the bookmark payload, so this note does not invent quotes from the essay.

Richard Seroter bookmarked it because the claim landed as a challenge: he had assumed quality could not be fully encoded as agent constraints, then conceded there is no reason that would be impossible.

## Key Takeaways

- Osmani's stated thesis: quality now depends on the constraints you set around your agents
- The practical fight is whether "good code" can be specified as harness rules, evals, and stop conditions rather than left as reviewer taste
- Treat this as a pointer to the Substack post, not a substitute for reading it
