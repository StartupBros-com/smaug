This agency playbook describes a data-driven system for producing founder content across X and LinkedIn from a 45-minute weekly interview. Rather than prompting a model afresh for each post, it proposes retaining client-specific knowledge and pairing specialist agents with senior strategists who turn performance data into a weekly content hypothesis. Its deal-flow, growth, and client-scale results are promotional claims from the author, not independently verified outcomes.

The proposed content engine joins five roles: a persistent client knowledge base, performance analysis, trend research, format selection, and brand-voice review. Each post is deliberately mapped to a topic, content pillar, perspective, and vehicle, then measured after publishing so the next week can favour formats and angles that reached the client’s intended audience.

## Key Takeaways

- A "Client Brain" is intended to retain a founder’s ICP, positioning, voice, content pillars, prior performance, and feedback as reusable context for future planning.
- The Performance, Trend Hunter, and Vehicle agents turn account-level results and niche conversations into testable weekly hypotheses about topics, angles, and formats.
- Strategists use the weekly interview to collect the founder’s own reasoning and stories, then convert them into a planned mix of posts rather than generic AI output.
- The playbook treats perspective and vehicle as separate decisions: the same topic can be a contrarian take, story, case study, or educational post and can be delivered through different formats.
- Its ICP model emphasizes business type, revenue range, audience language, and a specific online micro-community so content is optimized for relevant engagement rather than follower count alone.
