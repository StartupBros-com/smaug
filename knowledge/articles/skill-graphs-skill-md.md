This X Article argues that structured knowledge should be modeled as a graph rather than stored only in disconnected SKILL.md files. Its preview describes conventional skills as capturing one aspect of a task; the proposed alternative makes relationships between knowledge and capabilities available to an agent, supporting richer reuse and new kinds of applications.

## Key Takeaways

- Single-purpose skill files can preserve useful procedures, but they do not make dependencies and relationships explicit.
- Connecting knowledge into a graph can give agents more relevant context when they compose capabilities across tasks.
- The bookmark presents skill graphs as a practical next step for users who have already accumulated many Claude Code skills.
