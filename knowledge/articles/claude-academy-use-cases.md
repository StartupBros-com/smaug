Claude Academy's use-case library collects practical examples for applying Claude to research, writing, coding, analysis, and everyday work. It is intended as an idea-discovery resource for both solo practitioners and teams that want to translate general LLM capabilities into concrete workflows.

The preserved page response supplies the library's scope and description rather than readable individual use-case entries. Richard Seroter recommends it as a broad source of LLM-project inspiration, not as a prescriptive implementation guide.

## Key Takeaways

- The library spans research, writing, coding, analysis, and everyday tasks.
- Its examples are positioned for both individual and team workflows.
