Thinking Machines Lab describes work with Bridgewater to train a custom open model for the narrow but valuable task of deciding which financial documents warrant an analyst's attention. The approach uses expert-labeled examples and on-policy distillation through Tinker, aiming to reproduce expert filtering judgment more reliably and cheaply than general frontier LLMs.

## Key Takeaways

- Financial-document triage requires domain judgment that broad frontier models do not consistently supply.
- Expert-labeled training data lets a model target a concrete analyst workflow instead of only general language ability.
- The reported result shows a task-specific fine-tuned model outperforming frontier LLMs while reducing cost.
