Boris Cherny's framework addresses a recurring adoption gap: a small number of engineers may achieve major productivity gains with Claude before the rest of the organization has changed its practices. The linked artifact presents four steps for moving from those isolated wins toward broader team adoption; the prepared page data does not expose the framework's detailed step-by-step content.

## Key Takeaways

- AI adoption is an organizational change problem, not just an individual-tool proficiency problem.
- Individual productivity outliers can reveal both the opportunity and the need for deliberate rollout practices.
- The artifact is intended as a four-step guide to help companies extend successful AI use beyond early adopters.
