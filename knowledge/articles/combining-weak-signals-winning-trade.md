Roan presents an institutional-style framework for alpha combination: build a portfolio from numerous modestly predictive signals, then weight each by its estimated independent contribution and volatility rather than searching for a single decisive forecast. The article applies the approach to price, factor, volatility, mean-reversion, and market-microstructure signals, then adapts it to implied probabilities in prediction markets.

## Key Takeaways

- The Fundamental Law of Active Management frames risk-adjusted performance as average information coefficient times the square root of the number of genuinely independent signals; correlated signals do not provide the advertised breadth.
- The proposed 11-step process centers and normalizes historical signal returns, removes shared variance, estimates residual expected return, and assigns weights proportional to independent edge divided by volatility.
- For prediction markets, the framework combines several probability estimates, compares the aggregate estimate with market price, and recommends uncertainty-adjusted Kelly sizing with execution and order-flow monitoring.
