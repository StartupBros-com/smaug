Cloudflare introduces an AI-visibility reporting beta that frames answer-engine optimization as an observable extension of search visibility. Its proposed benchmark begins with likely prompts in a market category rather than a branded query, then records which sites an AI assistant cites, their placement in the response, and their prominence. This makes it possible to compare a site’s AI-recommendation presence against category competitors instead of merely checking whether a brand name appears.

The reporting is paired with AI Operator Activity: first-party traffic data broken down by operators such as OpenAI and Google. That view connects AI discovery to the underlying web operations — which bots crawl content, which refer users back, and whether they encounter access or navigation failures such as blocked requests or dead links. Together, the two views make AI visibility a combination of recommendation measurement, crawl accessibility, and referral outcomes.

## Key Takeaways

- Benchmark AI visibility with category-relevant prompts that do not name the brand being evaluated
- Track cited domains, their position in AI answers, and their relative prominence to understand competitive standing
- Pair model-facing recommendation data with real operator-specific crawl and referral telemetry
- Treat 403 responses and dead links as measurable obstacles to AI-agent access, not just ordinary website errors
- The reporting was announced as a beta with a registration-of-interest path
