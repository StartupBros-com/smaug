Barry Schwartz reported on 11 August 2026 that Search Console's generative-AI performance report — impressions for AI Overviews and AI Mode in Search and Discover — had appeared on every one of ~25 properties he could check, after a partial expansion a few weeks earlier. Google had not announced a full rollout. An update the same day walked that back: it is not on every domain yet, but it is on far more than before. John Mueller confirmed on Reddit that it is still not every domain, and like Discover/News it only renders when there is enough data. Google's own empty-state copy: the report hides if the site "hasn't received enough impressions in generative AI features on Google Search."

The report is an expandable tab under the main Performance report. Dimensions: impressions, pages, countries, devices (Search only), and dates (hourly through monthly). Still no queries and still no clicks — so it tells you *that* URLs appeared in AI features and *where*, not *for what* or whether anyone followed through. Hat tip to Vijay Chauhan, who flagged the wider availability on X.

For extracting the missing query side from the classic performance report, see the companion note on [How to Track Google AI Mode Traffic in Search Console](./how-to-track-google-ai-mode-traffic.md).

## Key Takeaways

- Generative-AI performance is now on most GSC properties, but Mueller says it is still not universal and is gated on sufficient impressions
- Metrics: impressions, pages, countries, devices, dates — no queries, no clicks
- Lives as an expandable tab under Performance, not a standalone product
- Absence of the tab can mean either not rolled out or not enough AI-feature impressions
