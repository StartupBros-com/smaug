The article presents a six-step operating system for Meta creative designed to prevent ad fatigue through a continuous research-and-iteration loop rather than a search for one permanent winning ad.

1. **Feed Claude raw customer language.** Consolidate reviews, surveys, and winning-ad evidence into exact quotes that reveal emotions, objections, desires, and awareness levels.
2. **Develop an angle.** Explore recurring pains, proven or untested messaging, and audience-awareness gaps with additional evidence from comments, reviews, Reddit, and competing ads.
3. **Choose creative formats.** Turn each angle into at least three format variations informed by internal performance, competitor activity and whitespace, and attention-holding organic content.
4. **Produce native-feeling ads.** Give strong creators a focused brief while preserving their natural delivery, then edit each execution to fit its audience and format.
5. **Test by concept.** Group format variations of one concept in an ad set, track spend alongside CPA, ROAS, and frequency, and respond to rising frequency with fresh upper-funnel angles.
6. **Compound winners.** Combine successful components, repackage proven angles across formats, test them with new creators and hooks, and use consistent Persona–Angle–Format–Hook tags to feed performance data back into Claude.

The central claim is that Meta's recommendation-driven delivery rewards a consistent, well-instrumented stream of customer-informed creative. A documented creative system lets teams prepare the next test before the current winner saturates, rather than starting research from scratch after every decline.
