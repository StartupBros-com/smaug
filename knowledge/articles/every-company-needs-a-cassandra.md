Sunil Pai proposes a "Cassandra": a background AI agent for the socially expensive work of organizational dissent. The accompanying announcement describes a worker people may otherwise dislike working with; @swyx highlights the idea as a way to create loops that themselves generate further loops.

## Key Takeaways

- The proposal gives AI a deliberately contrarian organizational role rather than another conventional task-execution role.
- Its value proposition is to make it easier to surface concerns that employees may avoid raising because the social cost is high.
