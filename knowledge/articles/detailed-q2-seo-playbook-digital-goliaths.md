Detailed Q2 is a long-form report examining companies that dominate Google search results. Glen Allsopp pairs the report with a writing-process note: he scores prospective insights for reader interest, then deliberately spreads the strongest material across the piece rather than letting its value steadily decline.

## Key Takeaways

- Score planned points for likely reader interest before ordering a long report or list post.
- Alternate high- and lower-interest sections so readers encounter fresh reasons to continue throughout the piece.
- Adjust the judgment for the intended audience, since the same insight can matter differently to local and ecommerce SEO readers.
- Treat the sequence as a testable editorial heuristic rather than a universal rule for long-form content.
