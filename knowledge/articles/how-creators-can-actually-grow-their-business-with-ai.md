# How Creators Can Actually Grow Their Business with AI

This interview argues that AI is most valuable as leverage for established business skills—not as a standalone business model. Rather than chase general-purpose SaaS or highly elaborate agent systems, the suggested opportunity is to use simple, repeatable AI workflows to improve one function for small businesses in a chosen vertical.

## Key ideas

- Sell a specific transformation, such as AI-assisted sales, accounting, marketing, or SEO/AEO operations, then reuse the underlying workflow across similar clients.
- Invest in ongoing multi-source research to build a differentiated knowledge base that gives agents better context and standards of judgment.
- Treat autonomy as earned: review agent output first, then delegate only the tasks that repeatedly require no corrections.
- Use AI to extract, structure, and scale a creator's thinking, while retaining editorial control over the final ideas and output.
- Build a background research system first—monitoring a niche, competitors, and trusted sources—because its daily briefing can supply content, offers, and outreach.
- Pair focused experimentation with business fundamentals such as sales, marketing, and operations; the interview contends these remain the primary differentiators.
