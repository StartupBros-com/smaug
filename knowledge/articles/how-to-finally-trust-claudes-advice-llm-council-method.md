Ole Lehmann describes a skill inspired by Karpathy's LLM Council method for making Claude’s advice more dependable. Rather than accepting one potentially agreeable answer, it convenes five AI advisers to argue over a question and anonymously review each other’s work, making disagreement and reasoning part of the decision process.

## Key Takeaways

- The method replaces a single-response workflow with a panel of five AI advisers who consider the same question.
- Anonymous cross-review is intended to expose weak reasoning and reduce the tendency for a model to tell a user what they want to hear.
