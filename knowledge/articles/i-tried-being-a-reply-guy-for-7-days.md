Noir Creates presents a self-reported experiment in high-volume replying on X. The method organizes target accounts by size and purpose, uses X lists or X Pro columns to reduce feed-scrolling, and aims for 100–300 replies per day. The article treats replies as a distribution channel: smaller accounts provide authentic participation, niche mid-sized accounts provide relevance, and large fast-moving accounts provide reach.

The operating rule is E.S.S.: reply early, format with spacing, and add a memorable contribution. The author used Grok for rough ideas and edited them into short replies, while cautioning that unaltered AI text can appear inauthentic. Reported impression gains—from four-digit daily results to millions of cumulative impressions—are anecdotal and should not be read as verified evidence about X's ranking system.

## Key Takeaways

- Segment accounts by audience size and objective rather than replying indiscriminately across the feed.
- Use lists or X Pro columns to make timely engagement repeatable without constant timeline switching.
- Early replies and readable formatting can matter as much as the substance of a comment in high-velocity conversations.
- Treat reported engagement outcomes as a single creator's case study, not a guaranteed or platform-endorsed growth tactic.
