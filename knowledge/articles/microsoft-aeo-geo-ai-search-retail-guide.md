Alex Groberman’s annotated walkthrough of Microsoft’s *From discovery to influence* guide argues that retailers must optimize not only for conventional search ranking but for AI systems that need to understand, trust, and act on product data. It distinguishes answer/agentic engine optimization (AEO), which makes data machine-readable, from generative engine optimization (GEO), which supplies credibility and citable evidence; the source also promotes the author’s SEO services.

## Key Takeaways

- AI shopping agents combine crawled-web reputation signals, structured product feeds and APIs, and live site data such as pricing, reviews, media, and checkout availability.
- Structured product fields including price, availability, variants, SKU, GTIN, and modification date help agents compare products reliably; the guide highlights Product, Offer, AggregateRating, Review, Brand, ItemList, and FAQ schema.
- Intent-oriented content should explain who a product is for, what problem it solves, and why it is preferable, then offer extractable elements such as comparisons, Q&A, feature lists, detailed alt text, and video transcripts.
- Trust signals—from verified reviews and expert coverage to certifications and consistent brand information—are presented as inputs to AI recommendations rather than optional marketing polish.
- The operational thesis is to treat feeds and live product data as strategic infrastructure: an agent cannot complete a purchase if the real site’s data, availability, or checkout path fails.
