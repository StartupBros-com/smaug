Anthropic announced an expanded partnership with Google and Broadcom intended to provide multiple gigawatts of next-generation compute. In the quoted announcement, Anthropic said its annualized revenue had surpassed $30 billion, up from $9 billion at the end of 2025, and positioned the additional capacity as necessary to meet accelerating demand for Claude.

FleetingBits uses that announcement to advance a broader market thesis: AI products can diffuse unusually quickly because a single human-shaped interface or API spans many use cases, and each capability advance opens distinct adoption curves. The post identifies Claude Code as one such curve, arguing that early positioning in coding agents can create durable user associations even when frontier models are otherwise comparable. These are the bookmark author's interpretations, not claims established by the announcement itself.

## Key Takeaways

- Anthropic describes the Google and Broadcom partnership as a source of multiple gigawatts of next-generation compute.
- The company said its revenue run rate exceeded $30 billion, compared with $9 billion at the end of 2025.
- The bookmarked analysis treats access to compute as a strategic constraint in frontier-lab competition.
- Coding agents are presented as a separate demand surface that can reset growth rates beyond the original chatbot market.
