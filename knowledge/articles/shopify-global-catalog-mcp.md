Shopify’s Global Catalog MCP documentation describes UCP catalog tools for searching across Shopify merchants, looking up products, and retrieving product details. The associated UCP deep-dive presents this as agent infrastructure: discovery, cart creation, and checkout can be negotiated against merchant capabilities rather than hard-coded per-store integrations.

## Key Takeaways

- The catalog tools expose cross-merchant product search and product-detail lookup through the Universal Commerce Protocol.
- UCP lets merchants declare supported operations and lets clients or agents negotiate the capability overlap needed for a transaction.
- The companion CLI can discover a merchant’s UCP profile, inspect available schemas, search catalog inventory, and begin cart or checkout workflows.
- The approach aims to replace scraping and bespoke integrations with structured, real-time commerce interactions that retain merchant-specific rules.
