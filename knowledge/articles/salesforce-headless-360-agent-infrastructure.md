This filing captures a VentureBeat link about Salesforce Headless 360 alongside Marc Benioff's announcement that Salesforce, Agentforce, and Slack capabilities are exposed through APIs, MCP, and CLI rather than a browser UI. The article body was unavailable in the prepared bookmark data, so the details here are limited to the announcement and accompanying commentary.

## Key Takeaways

- Benioff describes an API-first interface that lets agents access enterprise data, workflows, and tasks from Slack, voice, or other agent surfaces.
- Greg Isenberg frames headless enterprise SaaS as an opening for agent-native startups that use systems such as Salesforce, HubSpot, and Workday as backends rather than end-user interfaces.
- His thesis is that vertical workflow knowledge and distribution become durable advantages when implementation work is automated and pricing can be tied to outcomes instead of seats.
