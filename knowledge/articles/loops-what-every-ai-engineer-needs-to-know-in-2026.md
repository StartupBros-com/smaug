Rahul's primer argues that the important unit of AI engineering is an iterative loop rather than a one-off model call: a model's output becomes the next input, and the system evaluates and revises work until it meets the intended bar. The saved preview connects the argument to Peter Steinberger's advice that coding-agent work should be organized around loops, not individual prompts.

## Key Takeaways

- Reliable agentic work needs feedback and evaluation stages, not just a prompt and a generated answer.
- Treating outputs as new inputs enables a system to iterate toward a defined quality threshold.
- Designing the loop around an agent determines how it can recover from incomplete or incorrect intermediate work.
