Tal Raviv argues that product managers can create disproportionate leverage by deliberately redesigning how work reaches them and using AI as part of a broader productivity system. The article frames this approach as becoming a “super-IC”: an individual contributor who delivers impact beyond conventional expectations without moving into management.

## Key Takeaways

- Prevent or redirect work before it becomes a task, rather than optimizing an already overloaded to-do list.
- Replace some meetings with short Loom updates and reduce Slack overhead through selective attention and automation.
- Build a team that can operate without constant PM intervention, preserving time for higher-leverage discovery work.
- Use product scrapbooking to begin discovery earlier and let AI assist with drafting while retaining human judgment in reading and synthesis.
- Treat cognitive freshness as a resource to manage, alongside tools and processes.
