Borja's parasite-SEO playbook for domains too young to rank: publish buy-intent long tails on Medium, Substack, YouTube, Instagram, and GitHub, borrowing authority those hosts already have, instead of waiting years to compete with five-year-old #1 results. Ahrefs tracked a million new URLs for a year and only 1.74% cracked the top 10; 72.9% of current top-10 pages are more than three years old. Parasite SEO skips that fight. The claimed result is 20.3 million clicks in 12 months. The article is also onboarding for Distribb's Claude SEO skill.

The target queries are commercial, not volume: "best &lt;product&gt; for &lt;ICP&gt; in &lt;location&gt;", "how much does &lt;product&gt; cost for &lt;ICP&gt; in &lt;location&gt;", "&lt;product&gt; alternative for &lt;ICP&gt;". Low volume, high purchase intent, usually unwritten — you are the only answer, not the page trying to outrank Forbes.

## The seven steps

1. **Pull real questions from Search Console.** Filter Performance queries with a question-word regex (`^(who|what|where|when|why|how|which|can|do|does|is|are|should|will)\b`). These are the questions people already type, not the ones you assume.
2. **List 10–30 parent keywords** buyers would type into a chatbot (best payroll software, gusto alternative). Too competitive to win alone; they are the trunk.
3. **Cross with ICP and location.** `best <product> + for <ICP> + in <location>`. 31 heads × 86 verticals × 99 cities = 166,114 queries. Score so expensive verticals (personal injury, med spas) publish before cheap ones (yoga studios).
4. **Open accounts where AI already looks:** Medium, Substack, YouTube, Instagram, GitHub. You are renting their trust, not building a brand there.
5. **Use Claude routines plus browser use.** Nightly scheduled runs; browser use publishes to hosts with no API. Use a real API/scheduler when one exists.
6. **Write for SEO and GEO.** Quotable 3–5 sentence summary at the top (what answer engines lift), answer in the first paragraph, FAQ from the GSC questions, one image with the keyword as filename and alt, internal links on that account, and one statistic nobody else has — from your own billing, analytics, tickets, or reviews. Generic advice does not get cited; a number does.
7. **Hand Claude a nightly routine prompt** that generates the scored keyword universe, publishes one original article per account per night, tracks published keywords with a unique constraint so a crash cannot double-post, and feeds failures into the next run.

Two failure modes: the agent may report success on an unpublished draft because it built a URL from the editor page — verify the URL is actually public. And a failed run must return the keyword to the pool, not mark it used forever.

## Key Takeaways

- Do not fight aged domains on head terms; publish unwritten buy-intent long tails on already-trusted hosts
- Keyword universe is parent × ICP × city, scored by commercial value, not search volume
- Claude routines + browser use are what make parasite platforms without APIs automatable
- GEO formatting (quotable summary, first-paragraph answer, unique first-party stat) is what gets cited
- Trust-but-verify publish URLs; never burn a keyword on a failed login
