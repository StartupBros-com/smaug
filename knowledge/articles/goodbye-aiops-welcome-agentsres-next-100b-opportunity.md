Foundation Capital's thesis reframes AIOps as AgentSREs: AI agents that can take on reliability and operations work rather than merely assist with monitoring or analysis. Jaya Gupta's accompanying post argues that companies in this space need depth in machine learning, LLMs, and distributed systems, and presents the category as a large, still-open startup opportunity.

The captured page provides the article title and description but not a readable article body, so this filing is limited to the source metadata and the bookmark's stated investment thesis.

## Key Takeaways

- AgentSREs are positioned as a more ambitious successor to AIOps for production operations and observability.
- Building credible systems in the category requires expertise spanning ML, LLMs, and distributed systems.
- The bookmark frames the market as a potentially $100B-plus opportunity with room for multiple large companies.
