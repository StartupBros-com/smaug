Initial Commit's SEO Content Generation skill is a self-contained workflow for choosing, researching, fact-checking, writing, verifying, and registering one search-focused content piece per run. It bootstraps a brand and keyword foundation when none exists, then aims to produce content with genuine information gain that can rank in conventional search and be cited by AI answer engines.

## Key Takeaways

- The skill owns the whole editorial loop: decide on the next opportunity, research it, write a suitable content type, verify the result, and record it for future runs.
- It can choose among formats such as guides, comparisons, definitions, data studies, and case studies rather than forcing every opportunity into the same template.
- Its stated optimization target includes Google rankings as well as citation by answer engines such as ChatGPT and Perplexity.
