Tom Alder describes operating Strategy Breakdowns’ sponsorship business without a sales team by turning a personal sales process into more than 20 Claude Code skills. The system covers pipeline reviews, reply drafting, prospect research, outbound campaigns, proposals, ad copy, and renewals; each skill encodes specific qualification rules, pricing, tone, and follow-up cadence rather than generic prompting.

A scheduled daily workflow collects messages from Gmail and communications platforms through Beeper, reconciles senders with the CRM, drafts replies with confidence ratings, and surfaces stalled conversations in a local dashboard. Alder reviews and sends the proposed messages, reducing daily inbox processing from roughly two hours to 15 minutes while retaining human judgment for pricing and ambiguous negotiations.

The core compounding mechanism is an `/improve` workflow that compares Claude’s drafts with the final messages sent, extracts corrections, and updates the underlying skill files. Combined with CRM records, Notion data, conversation histories, voice guidance, pricing, and case studies, this gives each skill current business context instead of relying on a chat’s transient history.

## Key Takeaways

- Reusable skills can encode a specific operating process—including voice, qualification criteria, and follow-up rules—more reliably than one-off prompts.
- A unified dashboard can make AI assistance operational by preparing cross-channel work, detecting stale deals, and supplying strategic context before a person reviews it.
- Feedback from actual edits is the mechanism that makes the workflow improve over time, converting daily corrections into refinements to the process.
- Durable context from CRM, Notion, conversation records, pricing, and case studies is essential for sales automation that needs to act on current deal state.
