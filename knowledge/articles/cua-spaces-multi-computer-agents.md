Cua Spaces gives agents full desktops (VMs or containers) running on hardware you own, across your Macs, other computers or your own cloud. It is built on the open-source Cua Driver and Lume virtualization stack. @viticci used it to let Claude in T3 Code drive computer use on a second Mac over Tailscale.

## Key Takeaways

- A Space is a full desktop for agents that you can also watch; Cua does not host or rent machines.
- Free tier covers 2 computers in use and 20 remote hours a month; Pro is $18/month, Teams $30/seat/month.
- Requires macOS 26+ on Apple silicon for macOS Spaces; no account needed for local or by-address use.
- Viticci runs 8 Xcode simulators on a remote Mac to free RAM for local AI on his main machine.
