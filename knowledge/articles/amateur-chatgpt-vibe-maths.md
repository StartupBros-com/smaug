Scientific American reports on a 23-year-old without advanced mathematics training who used ChatGPT Pro while working on a decades-old Erdős problem. The account presents the result as an example of AI widening access to mathematical exploration, while Terence Tao suggests some long-standing difficulty may have been a mental block rather than an inherent barrier.

## Key Takeaways

- ChatGPT-assisted exploration helped an amateur engage with and solve a problem attributed to Erdős that had remained open for roughly 60 years.
- The reported method was unfamiliar to human experts, suggesting AI can help surface approaches outside conventional research habits.
- The story raises questions about how AI may change the boundary between formal training, conjecture generation, and mathematical discovery.
