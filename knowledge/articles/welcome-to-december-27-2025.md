A wide-ranging, highly speculative status report that frames late-2025 AI progress as the beginning of an intelligence explosion. It connects autonomous coding, internal reinforcement learning, scientific modeling, GPU-centric infrastructure, robotics, energy storage, and labor displacement into one accelerating technological narrative.

## Key Takeaways

- The article argues that persistent AI agents, autonomous coding systems, and research results on internal optimization show a transition from assisted work to recursively improving machine workflows.
- It links the perceived takeoff to capital-intensive infrastructure, including GPU-storage architectures, enormous data-center projects, and expanded investment in energy and semiconductor capacity.
- It projects second-order effects across education, knowledge work, institutions, robotics, medicine, and political regulation, while presenting those claims in an intentionally urgent, futurist register.
