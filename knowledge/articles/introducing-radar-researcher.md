Cloudflare introduces Radar Researcher, an AI-powered interface for examining global Internet trends and traffic data in natural language. Rather than requiring users to navigate a dashboard or write a specialized query, the tool accepts a plain-language research question and produces an interactive chart as the result.

The supplied bookmark contains the article title and page shell rather than the full article body, so this record is grounded in Cloudflare's announcement: Radar Researcher is built on Cloudflare's Developer Platform and is intended to make Cloudflare Radar's Internet data accessible through conversational exploration while keeping the underlying analysis visible as a chart.

## Key Takeaways

- Radar Researcher turns natural-language questions about Internet trends and traffic into interactive visual analysis.
- The product is positioned as an interface to global Internet data, not as a text-only research chatbot.
- Interactive charts give users an artifact to inspect, refine, and share after the AI interprets their question.
- Cloudflare built the tool on its Developer Platform, connecting the research experience to the company's existing data and developer infrastructure.
