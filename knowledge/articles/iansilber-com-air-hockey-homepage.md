Ian Silber, who leads design at OpenAI (previously Global Illumination, Artifact, Instagram), uses his personal homepage as a playful interactive piece instead of a conventional portfolio. His introduction is arranged in a circle, and the page is a 15-second air hockey game with a WebGL shader background, balloon squeeze and pop sounds, a score burst and an initials-signed leaderboard. It was bookmarked as an example of a novel take on what a website can be.

## Key Takeaways

- The "bio" is a circular text artwork, and the page doubles as a game with a goal, timer, score and end card.
- A hidden tweak panel (goal size, puck friction) is exposed on localhost or with a `?tweaks` query param.
- A leaderboard where players sign with three initials adds a social, arcade feel.
- Rauno Freiberg's point: a distinctive idea about what a site can be signals more craft than many AI-prompted side projects, but novelty is scarce.
