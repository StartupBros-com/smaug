Shopify CEO Tobi Lütke frames effective AI use as a fundamental expectation rather than an optional productivity experiment. The memo argues that AI can act as a thought partner, researcher, critic, tutor, pair programmer, and increasingly an executor of work, allowing teams to take on problems that would previously have been impractical.

## What This Means at Shopify

1. AI proficiency is expected of everyone because refusing to learn it risks professional stagnation as the tools become more central to each craft.
2. The prototype phase of every GSD project should be AI-heavy, using rapid experiments to create artifacts teammates can inspect and reason about.
3. Performance and peer reviews will include AI-use questions, treating prompting and contextualization as learned skills that benefit from feedback.
4. Employees are expected to learn independently and share successes and failures, with access to internal AI tools and dedicated time for integration.
5. Teams requesting more headcount or resources must first explain why AI agents could not accomplish the needed work.
6. The expectation applies to executives and the CEO as well as individual contributors.

## Core Argument

Lütke connects the policy to Shopify's "red queen" view of a fast-growing company: continuous improvement is necessary merely to remain competitive. AI is presented as an unusually large multiplier, capable of raising both individual contribution and the scope of work a team can credibly attempt. Shopify's broader task, in this framing, is to determine how entrepreneurship changes when AI is universally available and make its platform the best environment for businesses built in that world.
