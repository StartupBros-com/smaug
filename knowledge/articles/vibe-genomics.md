Vibe Genomics is a first-person, step-by-step account of setting up a home wet lab for whole-genome sequencing with no prior laboratory experience. The author’s stated goal is to keep genomic data out of third-party services; they report achieving approximately 16× coverage and comparing the result with 600,000 raw 23andMe SNP calls.

## Key Takeaways

- Home sequencing can be documented as a reproducible workflow rather than delegated to a commercial genetics provider.
- The author reports that the workflow kept their DNA at home and was more approachable than expected for a novice.
- Approximate 16× coverage and agreement with raw 23andMe SNP data provide a limited, self-reported cross-check of the result.
