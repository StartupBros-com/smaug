Aakash Gupta's summary of Marcus Moretti's guide describes an agent-native product-management system for a one-person product team. Instead of maintaining tickets, PRDs, sprints, and recurring status rituals, the approach holds strategy in a concise markdown file, uses a scheduled agent to assemble a daily evidence briefing from operating systems, and manages work on a simple Now/Next/Later board.

## Key Takeaways

- `strategy.md` records the target problem, intended customer, approach, measurable outcomes, and a small number of work tracks; the strategy interview is revisited periodically rather than converted into a backlog artifact.
- A daily `/ce:product-pulse` runs at 8 a.m., reads product, revenue, observability, and database signals, and writes a short briefing that replaces routine status collection.
- The work board is intentionally narrow: Now, Next, and Later, with In Progress and Done states instead of sprints, standups, or backlog grooming.
- The model makes machine-readable integrations a procurement constraint: systems without an MCP or equivalent agent-accessible interface become difficult for the PM and its agents to use.
- The guide recasts the PM as an operator who directs an evidence-informed product loop while agents handle much of the administrative coordination work.
