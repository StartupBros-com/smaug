A free directory of 500 copy-paste workflows and prompts for Muse (the AI assistant), covering sales, marketing, ops, finance, customer success, hiring, fundraising, engineering, and product teams. Each workflow is submitted by someone who does the job, and the site pitches Muse as more than an admin assistant.

## Key Takeaways

- Includes an 11-workflow "Start here" set (e.g. pre-call prospect brief, outreach follow-up, campaign brief, vendor onboarding)
- Searchable directory; a few opens are free and a free account unlocks unlimited access
- Community submissions: users can submit their own prompt, setup, and outcome
