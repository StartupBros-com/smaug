Daniel presents an AI UGC operating model that separates consistent script generation from the harder problem of distribution. The proposed writing system uses a durable persona brief, a source map for approved product claims and evidence, and batched variations of hooks, lengths, and calls to action. This shared context is intended to prevent the generic voice, factual drift, and contradictory output associated with isolated one-shot prompts.

The article argues that increasing generation capacity alone does not create market reach: a solo operator can produce many scripts but cannot independently create, publish, review, and learn from hundreds of varied videos across accounts and platforms. Its commercial conclusion is that brands need a creator-distribution network as well as a well-specified creative system; the final sections promote AffiliateNetwork as that layer.

## Key Takeaways

- Define persona constraints, especially phrases and behaviors the character would never use, to reduce generic AI copy.
- Maintain a source map that connects each marketing claim to supporting evidence before script generation begins.
- Generate a batch of variations against the same persona and evidence rather than restarting from an empty chat for every script.
- Treat creative quality and distribution capacity as separate constraints: more capable prompting does not by itself expand reach.
- Evaluate AI UGC systems by their feedback loop across production, publishing, measurement, and iteration, not only by script output.
