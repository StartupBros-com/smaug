Cloudflare introduces its Agent Readiness score and a public scanner intended to show site owners how effectively AI agents can discover and use their sites. The captured page description identifies the relevant emerging surfaces as robots.txt and Markdown negotiation, llms.txt, MCP, OAuth, agent skills, and agentic commerce.

The bookmark also records a useful criticism: Gagan Ghotra finds the metrics insufficiently clear and notes that Cloudflare's own site appears to need improvement under the scanner. That makes the announcement valuable both as a practical checklist and as an example of the still-unsettled criteria for evaluating agent-facing web experiences.

## Key Takeaways

- Agent readiness spans discovery files, machine-readable content, authenticated interfaces, and task-specific agent capabilities rather than a single metadata file.
- The scanner provides a concrete way to inspect a site against these emerging standards, but its score should be examined alongside the underlying checks and their weighting.
- Public diagnostics can accelerate adoption of agent-facing conventions while also exposing how immature or ambiguous those conventions remain.
