# Small Bets P&L — Mar 2024

Daniel Vassallo shared this spreadsheet as the detailed P&L behind Small Bets’ Q1 2024 update. The accompanying update reports $173K in revenue and $117K in profit, with a $1,500 high-touch Launchpad upsell contributing $84K across 57 seats.

The business added 533 paid members to surpass 5,000 total, while average lifetime revenue per member rose from $208 to $220. Vassallo also describes opening three class recordings as a free trial to turn existing site traffic into a new acquisition funnel.
