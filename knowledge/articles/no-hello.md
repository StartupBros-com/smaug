The “no hello” rule asks people to send their actual request, relevant context, and question in their first chat message rather than opening with a greeting and waiting for a response. It eliminates avoidable back-and-forth, makes requests answerable when recipients are available, and supports clearer asynchronous collaboration.

Ben Lang notes that the convention was part of Notion’s first internal communication guidelines and recommends adopting it across startup teams to make Slack use more effective.

## Key Takeaways

- Include the request and necessary context with the greeting so the recipient can act immediately.
- Avoiding greeting-only messages removes a blocking wait cycle for both sender and recipient.
- The convention makes chat communication more compatible with asynchronous work and time-zone differences.
