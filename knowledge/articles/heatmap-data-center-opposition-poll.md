Heatmap News reports a new Heatmap Pro poll, conducted by Embold Research, showing that US public support for AI data centers has continued to collapse since the spring. Three-quarters of Americans now say they would oppose a data center being built near where they live. Note: only the page metadata and the author's tweet thread were extracted, not the full article body.

## Key Takeaways

- 75% of Americans say they would oppose a local data center, and 61% say they would "strongly oppose" it (per the author's tweet)
- The question wording has not changed across polling waves: a year ago Americans were split 43-42 support/oppose, and in February a bare majority opposed
- The author calls it a swift, massive shift in public opinion; the article adds party ID and rural/urban breakdowns
- Article keywords point to state-level political angles (Kathy Hochul, Greg Abbott) and water concerns
