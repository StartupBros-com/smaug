Citrini frames its annual set of 26 trades as a thematic watchlist rather than a point forecast: a way to generate differentiated scenarios, surface overlooked risks, and identify ideas worth monitoring. The post emphasizes that the basket mixes prospective winners with hedges and lower-conviction themes, so it is not a single directional market call.

## Key Takeaways

- The stated purpose is broad idea generation and blind-spot reduction, not pretending to predict the year with certainty.
- The selections span different levels of conviction, including potential winners, scenario hedges, and themes to track.
- The prior annual list had more than half its ideas beat the S&P 500 and more than 80% finish positive, according to the author.
- Drones, Ukraine normalization, and South Africa relative to Turkey were among the prior list's strong themes, while remittances disappointed.
- The prior single-stock selections averaged a 26.5% return, more than ten percentage points above the S&P 500.
- The actual 2026 trade list is subscriber-only, so this entry records the available framing rather than inferring the paywalled selections.
