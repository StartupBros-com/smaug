YC S26 launch post for Omanta, a per-patient cancer research lab. The claim is that the bottleneck is not missing science but missing *attention*: most doctors have about 20 minutes per visit while discoveries land every day and the average bench-to-bedside lag is ~17 years. Even the hardest cases rarely have anyone whose full-time job is to interrogate every layer of that one person's disease and push the newest options toward treatment.

Omanta integrates a patient's complete medical record and personal genomics with current clinical evidence, models the molecular drivers, and maps existing and emerging therapies against that biology. If the right treatment does not exist, the lab launches a personalized campaign to build it — cancer vaccines, immunotherapies, targeted radioligand therapies, CAR-T, drug repurposing, expanded access, and continuous molecular monitoring.

The proof case is GitLab co-founder Sid Sijbrandij's complete remission. CEO Alfredo Gonzalez, PhD (UCLA bioinformatics; Broad Cancer Dependency Map; Harvard Wagers Lab) was genomics lead on that year-long campaign: 25TB of sequencing, gaps in existing genomic reports, and actionable findings that reshaped the therapeutic stack, including personalized mRNA/DNA vaccines. He had previously built one of the largest centralized multi-omic datasets for rare tumors. CSO Ranad Humeidi, PhD finished a Harvard chemical-biology doctorate in three years (a druggable ulcerative-colitis mechanism that became a multimillion-dollar GSK collaboration), then directed a single-patient rare-cancer program and later scientific strategy for 12+ medically complex patients after standard options ran out. The two met eight years earlier on CRISPR cancer work at the Broad.

The ask is intros to patients facing difficult diagnoses and physicians who want a dedicated research team behind their hardest cases. Company site is [omanta.com](https://omanta.com/). Related prior capture: [Sid Sijbrandij's own treatment writeup](./sid-sijbrandij-cancer-osteosarcoma-personalized-medicine.md).

## Key Takeaways

- Product is a research lab organized around one patient, not a clinic or a drug
- Records + personal genomics + live evidence → molecular model → map or *build* the therapy
- Founders' flagship is Sid Sijbrandij's remission campaign (vaccines, CAR-T, expanded access, monitoring)
- Stated gap: 20-minute visits vs a ~17-year translation lag, not a shortage of papers
