Pieter Levels' interactive, explicitly subjective map of technology and business trends turns his personal observations about being early to shifts such as digital nomadism into an evolving visual reference. Rather than claim predictive certainty, the page presents a maintained “brain dump” whose individual trend lines can be explored and compared.

## Key Takeaways

- The chart records one entrepreneur's changing beliefs about overlapping trends rather than a formal forecast.
- Readers can select individual lines, enter fullscreen, and switch to an indexed view to examine the timeline from different perspectives.
- Its value is in making a qualitative trend thesis inspectable and updateable as new information arrives.
