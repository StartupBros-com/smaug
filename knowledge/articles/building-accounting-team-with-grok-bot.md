SpaceXSI describes giving five Grok Bots full-time roles in its accounting team across the order-to-cash process, onboarding and managing them like human teammates. The post doubles as a playbook for automating a business process with AI agents.

## Key Takeaways

- Five bots, each owning one well-scoped existing process end to end: Provisioning, Billing, Billing Support, Cash Applications, and Collections.
- Processes were made reliable first, using internal apps that define permitted actions and keep an audit log; each Bot has a Notion operating manual that only process owners can edit.
- Permissions are limited: low-risk tasks run independently, while credits and refunds need designated human approval.
- Roughly three-week onboarding: week 1 observe and build the knowledge base, week 2 propose actions with human approval, week 3 run routine work and escalate exceptions. The customer-facing Collections Bot took about three months because of security needs such as checking sender emails against approved contacts.
- Reported results: invoice issuance from about 24 hours to minutes, about eight hours a day freed by the Billing Support Bot, cash application from 3-4 hours to about 30 minutes.
- Lessons: a poorly defined or undocumented process will not be fixed by handing it to a Bot, and Bots need documentation, coaching, and clear approval controls.
