This X Article presents Hermes as a persistent local agent powered by Grok through a SuperGrok/X Premium+ subscription. It distinguishes a basic research configuration—Hermes with Grok OAuth and X/web search—from an optional power setup using xurl and an X developer app to reach private data such as bookmarks, timelines, and profile information.

The practical focus is a repeatable research system rather than one-off prompts: collect material, filter weak or duplicated sources, map themes and disagreements, verify important claims against primary sources, synthesize a brief, and retain useful context. The author suggests turning repeatable procedures into Hermes skills and recurring intelligence tasks into cron jobs, while warning that `degraded: true` X-search responses are not actual X-index results and should not be cited as such.

## Key Takeaways

- Start with Hermes, Grok OAuth, and working X/web search before adding X developer credentials or messaging automation.
- Treat xurl as an optional layer for authenticated access to personal X data and verify the selected app/profile to avoid authorization failures.
- Ask the agent to surface conflicts, source important claims, and distinguish confirmed information from speculation.
- Promote durable standards to memory, multi-step workflows to skills, and validated recurring tasks to scheduled jobs.
