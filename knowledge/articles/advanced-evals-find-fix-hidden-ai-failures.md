Sequel to "Building eval systems that improve your AI product", written for Lenny's Newsletter by Hamel Husain and Shreya Shankar. Its thesis is that teams should never skip error discovery before writing evals. It draws on their work with 50+ AI companies. Only the page metadata and Lenny's announcement tweet were available, so this summary is limited to those.

## Key Takeaways

- Never skip error discovery: look at real traces to find failure modes before building evals.
- The piece covers what to automate and what not to automate in an eval workflow.
- A free plugin (the evals-skills repo) lets a coding agent do most of the heavy lifting.
- Lenny cites eval payoffs: Ramp receipt collection 35% to 83% accuracy, Shopify workflow builder 2.2x faster and 68% cheaper, Harvey contract reviewer quality nearly doubled, Cursor Auto Balance routing 41% cheaper with higher satisfaction.
