Billion Dollar Tweets is a directory of tweets, threads, and articles that the creator considers the most valuable ideas shared on Twitter. Its premise is that many of the smartest people share useful insights there, but the volume of content and shifting algorithms bury them. Every entry is hand-picked and reviewed by a human before being added. At capture time the homepage listed 98 tweets, filterable by category.

## Key Takeaways

- Categories include AI, Career, Creative, Distribution, Health, Learning, Misc, Open Source, Software, Startups, UI, and Wealth
- Largest categories at capture: Distribution (20), Misc (18), Career (16), Startups (15)
- The site accepts tweet submissions and suggestions via forms, and has about, manifesto, and newsletter pages
- Promotes opensox.ai, the creator's open source career product
