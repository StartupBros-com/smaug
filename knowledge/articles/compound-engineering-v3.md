Compound Engineering v3 is a breaking release that standardizes its skill and agent namespace around `ce-` prefixes and adds traceable requirements artifacts from brainstorming through planning, implementation, testing, and review. The release also broadens native support across Claude Code, Codex, Pi, and Copilot while tightening review and debugging workflows.

## Key Takeaways

- Stable requirement, acceptance-example, and implementation-unit IDs create a provenance chain from an initial idea to code, tests, and review findings.
- The `ce-` naming scheme resolves collisions among plugin skills and avoids Windows filesystem issues caused by colon-prefixed names.
- Review tools shift from bucket-level decisions to per-finding engagement, while debugging gains environment checks, assumption audits, and broader investigation guidance.
- Native installation and runtime support across several coding-agent harnesses replaces fragile compatibility layers and improves parallel-agent behavior.
