Khairallah AL-Awady presents a six-phase playbook for starting an AI-automation services business. Its premise is that businesses have recurring, process-driven work but lack the ability to turn Claude, context design, MCP servers, and scheduled workflows into dependable operations. The course recommends beginning with a toolkit, selecting a narrow market, and using a free first implementation to produce a measured case study before selling a packaged service.

The commercial strategy is outcome-led rather than hourly: map the automation to recurring time savings, price the build as a share of the resulting value, and use evidence from the first client to acquire similar buyers. As common workflows emerge within a niche, standardize them into reusable packages and offer ongoing maintenance, turning bespoke implementation work into recurring revenue.

## Key Takeaways

- Build capability first through context-file design, reliable skill files, MCP integrations, and scheduled workflows that handle missing data gracefully.
- Select a niche whose workflows are familiar, repeatable, valuable enough to fund a project, and not likely to be automated internally by a technical team.
- Earn a first case study by automating one client’s most painful recurring task, then measure manual time, automated time, quality changes, and weekly savings.
- Package discovery, custom skills, context, integrations, testing, documentation, and launch support around business outcomes instead of billing for implementation hours.
- Acquire comparable clients through demonstrable content, focused outreach, and referrals; later productize repeated patterns and sell ongoing optimization or maintenance.
