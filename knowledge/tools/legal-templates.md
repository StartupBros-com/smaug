General Legal’s public repository provides attorney-drafted legal documents for startups and technology companies under the CC0 1.0 license. It supplies both LLM-optimized Markdown and original Word documents, with highlighted fields that need company-specific customization.

## Key Features

- Covers master services agreements, advisor agreements, employment offer letters, NDAs, privacy policies, terms of use, cookie notices, and data-processing addenda.
- Includes U.S.-focused and GDPR-enhanced privacy documents plus U.S. and global data-processing agreements.
- Publishes editable Markdown alongside original `.docx` files, making the templates practical for legal review and agent-assisted drafting.
