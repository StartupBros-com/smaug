Timeline Visualizer turns an exported Google Maps Timeline file (Timeline.json) into an animated travel video. You pick exact dates or a month range, preview the journey on an animated map, and render an MP4 to watch or share. The author (@mahler83) says an earlier draft was started with Antigravity and finished with Codex, and shared the APK for Android users.

## Key Features

- Android app (8.0+), installable from Google Play, open testing, or the APK on the GitHub releases page
- iPhone web app that runs in Safari with no upload of the Timeline file
- Trip selection by saved trip, monthly or yearly recap, or exact dates, with video length from 10 to 300 seconds
- Local Travel Journal that keeps journey history and merges newer exports
- Guide for restoring a missing Google Maps Timeline from an encrypted backup
