Tremor is an open-source, self-hostable real-time monitor for prediction markets. It tracks active Polymarket contracts, ranks meaningful price movements on a 0–10 intensity scale, and presents changes across 5-minute, 1-hour, and 24-hour windows so users can investigate emerging market signals quickly.

## Key Features

- Monitors roughly 500 active Polymarket markets, applying price-change and volume thresholds to reduce noise from low-liquidity activity.
- Uses a Convex-backed pipeline to collect price snapshots, calculate intensity scores, and stream live results to a Next.js dashboard.
- Provides multi-timeframe analysis and AI-powered market-context explanations; the project is community-maintained and self-hostable.
