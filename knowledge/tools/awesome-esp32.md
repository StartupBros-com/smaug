Hand-picked list of ESP32 projects that actually run, not a dump of every library. Every Applications entry links to a working repository; demo links stay when the demo is the point. Maintained by @sylvechv / s0lness, with CONTRIBUTING.md and an ideas/ backlog for things that still need to be built.

Two top-level sections: **Applications** (things people have flashed and used) and **Tools, utilities & libraries** (what you build them with). Two field guides sit beside the list: general ESP32 development practices, and a Waveshare AMOLED 1.8 guide — a lot of the "creative & play" entries target that one pocket touchscreen (IMU, speaker, battery), so one board runs tinydraw, infinite-golf, esp32-gameos, fluidbox, and similar toys.

## Notable Applications

- **Companions & AI:** chat-stick (hold-to-talk Gemini Live on an ESP32-S3), xiaozhi-esp32 (MCP chatbot firmware / desk-companion ecosystem), pixelcat and pocket-pet (Tamagotchi-style AMOLED pets)
- **Displays & ambient:** awtrix3 (scriptable Ulanzi pixel clock), HomePoint (MQTT/HomeKit wall screen)
- **E-paper:** TRMNL firmware, OpenEPaperLink (shelf-label mesh), InkSight (24-mode desk companion)
- **Home:** Tasmota, WLED
- **Audio:** squeezelite-esp32 (AirPlay/Spotify/Bluetooth endpoint)
