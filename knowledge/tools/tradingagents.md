TradingAgents is an LLM-based trading-firm simulation organized around distinct agent roles. The bookmark describes researchers, risk managers, and decision-makers debating a proposed trade before a position is opened, making it a reference architecture for multi-agent financial analysis rather than a single-model stock-picking workflow.

## Key Features

- Separates market research, risk management, and trading decisions across specialized agents
- Uses an internal debate before each trade to surface conflicting reasoning and risk views
- Represents the leading project in a wider finance-tool roundup that also includes research, quant, and prediction-market repositories
