Jeffrey Emanuel's Claude skill for assembling a music video from a finished audio track plus a pile of iPhone clips of someone singing along to that same track playing on the phone. The agent aligns the clips to the audio, categorizes them, and cut-fuses them into one edit. The page scrape at ingest was an empty Next.js shell, so this note is from the announcement tweet only.

The design claim is feedback without a full re-render: you mark what you dislike and the agent makes surgical recuts instead of redoing the heavy alignment work. Using the skill before you shoot also gets capture tips (how to record so the later alignment has something to work with). Emanuel is explicit that a human in Premiere for days will still win, but this is the low-effort path that walks the whole process.

## Key Features

- Input: studio/audio track + same-phone singalong iPhone videos
- Align, categorize, and fuse clips with cuts
- Incremental feedback so recuts skip the expensive alignment pass
- Optional pre-shoot guidance if you invoke the skill before recording
