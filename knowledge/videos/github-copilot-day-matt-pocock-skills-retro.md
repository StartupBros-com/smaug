## Key Takeaways

- Flow: grill-with-docs (pre-work alignment questioning) then implement, with review agents acting as the refactor step of red-green-refactor
- Larger work: improve-codebase-architecture produces a report, which becomes a spec plus tickets; implement-spec maps ticket dependencies, opens a draft PR, works independent tickets in parallel worktrees, rebases, and code-reviews
- Shout-outs: Dexter Horthy's "show me" skill for diagrams and HTML artifacts in PRs, and a de-slop skill that removes AI writing tells
- /retro reads the most recent sessions, checkpoints and artifacts and suggests fixes to skills, tool token economy and navigation (e.g. tightening implement-spec's base-branch resolution)
