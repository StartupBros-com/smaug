Nick Gray's hotel-review playlist, attached as the example when he told Pieter Levels to film short YouTube walkthroughs of properties. Gray says a few years of these videos pull about $6k/year because hotel/travel queries carry high CPM. The captured transcript is the Sensei Lānaʻi Four Seasons (Hawaii) tour, filmed 9 March 2022, unsponsored, walk-and-talk style.

## Sensei Lānaʻi walkthrough

Gray walks the estate at golden hour and again in the morning: landscaped grounds, a claimed $100M+ sculpture collection from Larry Ellison (Boteros including Adam and Eve, a Jeff Koons in the lobby, a Balthazar, a large orchid), Nāu by Sensei, fireplaces and chairs in the lobby, sushi bar and restaurant peek, Garden Bar, grab-and-go breakfast, and what he calls his favorite hotel library — curated new non-fiction and coffee-table books plus chess and high-end dominoes, not leftover paperbacks.

Room tour: king bed, touchscreen controls, full floor-to-ceiling blackout shades, balcony over the gardens, large TV and seating area, Nespresso and fridge, single-sink bath, bathrobes, and Toto washlets (flush on top; he notes his mother got confused). Pool is a U/horseshoe with private cabana-style seats and a banyan tree; the hotel can be nearly full without feeling crowded. Gym: four Pelotons, free weights, kettlebells, rowers, treadmills, Smith machine, lockers, filtered-water stations around the property. Further back, Japanese-style onsins — warm private hot tubs with waterfall noise and jungle views, plus benches, not for swimming.

Practical notes from the tape: book the botanist walk (David) in advance — listed as full at six people, four no-shows that day; some imported plants "cost as much as a house"; birds and butterflies throughout; mask mandates were about to lift in March 2022. He points viewers to a separate blog post mapping the sculptures ("Nick Gray Sensei Lai"). Closes by asking people planning a visit what they are looking forward to.
